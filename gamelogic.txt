Strike3 — Complete Game Rules

================================

BOARD GEOMETRY
--------------

The board is a horizontal rectangle with a 3:2 aspect ratio (600×400 viewBox).
Nine positions are arranged in a 3×3 grid at these exact coordinates:

  1 (40, 40)       2 (300, 40)       3 (560, 40)
  4 (40, 200)      5 (300, 200)      6 (560, 200)
  7 (40, 360)      8 (300, 360)      9 (560, 360)

Visual layout:

  1 ───── 2 ───── 3
  │ \     │     / │
  │   \   │   /   │
  4 ───── 5 ───── 6
  │   /   │   \   │
  │ /     │     \ │
  7 ───── 8 ───── 9

All lines are straight. The board has sharp rectangular corners (no rounded corners).
Positions lie on the line intersections, not inside cells.

BOARD LINES (8 straight lines)
------------------------------

Horizontal (3):
  1–2–3   (top row)
  4–5–6   (middle row)
  7–8–9   (bottom row)

Vertical (3):
  1–4–7   (left column)
  2–5–8   (middle column)
  3–6–9   (right column)

Diagonal (2):
  1–5–9   (descending: top-left to bottom-right)
  3–5–7   (ascending: top-right to bottom-left)

CONNECTIONS (16 adjacent pairs)
-------------------------------

Consecutive pairs from the board lines:

Horizontal:
  1–2, 2–3, 4–5, 5–6, 7–8, 8–9

Vertical:
  1–4, 4–7, 2–5, 5–8, 3–6, 6–9

Diagonal:
  1–5, 5–9, 3–5, 5–7

Adjacency list (undirected):
  1: [2, 4, 5]
  2: [1, 3, 5, 8]
  3: [2, 5, 6]
  4: [1, 5, 7]
  5: [1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9]  (center connects to all)
  6: [3, 5, 9]
  7: [4, 5, 8]
  8: [2, 5, 7, 9]
  9: [5, 6, 8]

IMPORTANT: Nodes that appear close geometrically are NOT connected unless
a board line explicitly connects them. For example:
  • 1 is NOT connected to 3 (must go through 2)
  • 1 is NOT connected to 6, 7, 8
  • 4 is NOT connected to 2, 3, 6, 8
  • 2 is NOT connected to 4, 6, 7, 9
  • 3 is NOT connected to 1, 4, 7, 8

INITIAL SETUP
-------------

Player 1 (Blue/Black): Three pieces at positions 1, 2, 3 (top row)
Player 2 (Red/White):  Three pieces at positions 7, 8, 9 (bottom row)
Positions 4, 5, 6 (middle row) start empty.

Player 1 (Blue) moves first.

MOVEMENT RULES
--------------

1. ONE-STEP MOVEMENT
   A piece may move to any directly connected node (per adjacency list)
   if the destination is unoccupied.

2. TWO-STEP MOVEMENT
   A piece may move exactly two steps along a SINGLE STRAIGHT LINE if:
   a) The start, intermediate, and destination nodes lie on the same
      defined board line (one of the 8 lines above)
   b) The path follows two consecutive connections from that line
   c) The INTERMEDIATE node is UNOCCUPIED (no jumping)
   d) The DESTINATION node is UNOCCUPIED
   e) Movement proceeds in one direction (no turning corners)

   Valid two-step moves (with intermediate node):
   From 1:  1→3 via 2    (horizontal)
            1→7 via 4    (vertical)
            1→9 via 5    (diagonal)
   From 2:  2→8 via 5    (vertical)
   From 3:  3→1 via 2    (horizontal)
            3→9 via 6    (vertical)
            3→7 via 5    (diagonal)
   From 4:  4→6 via 5    (horizontal)
   From 5:  (none — all lines end at outer nodes)
   From 6:  6→4 via 5    (horizontal)
   From 7:  7→1 via 4    (vertical)
            7→9 via 8    (horizontal)
            7→3 via 5    (diagonal)
   From 8:  8→2 via 5    (vertical)
   From 9:  9→7 via 8    (horizontal)
            9→3 via 6    (vertical)
            9→1 via 5    (diagonal)

3. NO JUMPING
   The intermediate node in a two-step move MUST be empty.
   This applies regardless of which player owns the blocking piece.

4. OCCUPIED DESTINATIONS
   A piece may never move onto an occupied node (own or opponent).

5. NO ARBITRARY GEOMETRIC MOVEMENT
   A move is legal ONLY if it follows the defined board lines.
   Proximity, Euclidean distance, or apparent diagonals do not create connections.
   Examples of INVALID moves:
   • 4 → 2 (not on same line, would require turning corner)
   • 1 → 6 (no straight-line path exists)
   • 3 → 8 (not on same line)
   • Any move that changes direction mid-path

TURN STRUCTURE
--------------

1. Click one of your pieces to select it.
2. Legal destinations highlight (green for one-step, blue for two-step).
3. Click a highlighted destination to move.
3. Turn passes to opponent.
4. Repeat until win or draw.

VICTORY CONDITION — STRIKE3 SPECIAL RULE
----------------------------------------

A player wins ONLY when all three of their pieces occupy a straight line
that passes through the CENTER position (5).

There are EXACTLY FOUR winning lines:

  4–5–6   (middle horizontal row)
  2–5–8   (middle vertical column)
  1–5–9   (main diagonal: top-left to bottom-right)
  3–5–7   (anti-diagonal: top-right to bottom-left)

The center position (5) MUST contain one of the winning player's pieces.

The following are NOT winning lines, even with three pieces:
  ✗ 1–2–3   (top row)
  ✗ 7–8–9   (bottom row)
  ✗ 1–4–7   (left column)
  ✗ 3–6–9   (right column)

Only the four lines through the center count.

DRAW CONDITION
--------------

Game ends in a draw if all 9 positions are occupied and no player has won.

RESTART
-------

After a win or draw, a "Restart" button appears.
Clicking it resets the board to the initial state with Player 1 (Blue) to move.

EXAMPLES
--------

Valid one-step moves from initial position:
  Blue at 1 → 4 or 5
  Blue at 2 → 5 or 8
  Blue at 3 → 5 or 6
  Red at 7 → 4 or 5
  Red at 8 → 2 or 5
  Red at 9 → 5 or 6

Valid two-step moves (when intermediate and destination empty):
  Blue: 1→3 (via 2), 1→7 (via 4), 1→9 (via 5)
  Red:  9→7 (via 8), 9→3 (via 6), 9→1 (via 5)

Winning positions for Blue:
  Blue at 4, 5, 6  → WIN (middle horizontal)
  Blue at 2, 5, 8  → WIN (middle vertical)
  Blue at 1, 5, 9  → WIN (main diagonal)
  Blue at 3, 5, 7  → WIN (anti-diagonal)

Non-winning (even with 3 pieces):
  Blue at 1, 2, 3  → NO WIN (top row)
  Blue at 7, 8, 9  → NO WIN (bottom row)
  Blue at 1, 4, 7  → NO WIN (left column)
  Blue at 3, 6, 9  → NO WIN (right column)

Same rules apply to Red player.
